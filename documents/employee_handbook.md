# Employee Handbook / 员工手册

## Parental Leave / 育儿假

Eligible full-time employees receive sixteen weeks of paid parental leave after birth, adoption, or foster placement. Employees should notify Human Resources at least thirty calendar days before planned leave when practicable. Benefits continue during approved paid leave.

符合条件的全职员工在子女出生、收养或寄养安置后可享受十六周带薪育儿假。在可行情况下，员工应至少提前三十个日历日通知人力资源部门。批准的带薪休假期间，员工福利继续有效。

## Remote Work / 远程办公

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00-15:00 Pacific Time. Confidential information must only be accessed through company-managed devices and the corporate VPN.

经经理批准，员工每周最多可以远程办公三天。核心协作时间为太平洋时间 10:00-15:00。机密信息只能通过公司管理的设备和企业 VPN 访问。

## Information Classification / 信息分类

Internal data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Restricted data must be encrypted in transit and at rest. Suspected data exposure must be reported to Security Operations within one hour.

内部数据分为公开、内部、机密和受限四个级别。受限数据在传输和静态存储时都必须加密。疑似数据泄露必须在一小时内报告给安全运营团队。
